MaterialCraft 0.1.2 — first integration test

Procedurally generated in the installed MaterialCraft application from its
`Polished_marble` sample graph. Exported through the application UI as six
1024×1024, 8-bit PNG maps. No AI generation credits were used for these maps.

Application and sample source: https://github.com/storytold/materialcraft
MaterialCraft is offered under MIT or Apache-2.0 by the ArtCraft Team (2026).
Its upstream README states that the material samples are procedural rather
than third-party photos, scans or texture assets. No ArtCraft trademarks or
application icons are incorporated into the game artwork.

This material is used only by the optional pedestal in the visual laboratory.
The world materials are unchanged by this integration test. Mobile rendering
and normal-map orientation still require visual verification.
